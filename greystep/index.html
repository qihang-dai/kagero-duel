THE BASALT WARDEN
A reactive turn-based boss fight · 3D

Greystepvs. the Basalt Warden

After the bell fell silent, Aldric, Wren and Sable took the north road. At Greystep the pass is held by a warden of living basalt, built from the same stone as the cliffs around it.

Its turn
J parry right on impact · K dodge · Space jump its quakes. Parry or jump a whole string to counter.
Your turn
1–4 or ↑↓ + J to choose, K back. Press J as a skill’s ring closes.
Aim
Mouse or arrows, J / click fires (1 AP). Shoot the blue crystals to strip its shields. The glowing core takes extra damage.
Story widens the timing windows and shows rings. Gamepad: A parry/confirm · B dodge/back · Y jump. M mutes. Also: Pale Hour (the pixel version) · Kagerō.
